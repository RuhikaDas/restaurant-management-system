# Restaurant Management System

## Overview

The Restaurant Management System is a relational database solution designed to **centralize and manage restaurant operations** across ordering, kitchen workflows, menu management, table allocation, employee records, customer feedback, billing and delivery.

## Key Features

### 1. Order Management

* Maintains customer orders and order details.
* Supports digital order processing and billing workflows.
* Connects order information with kitchen operations.

### 2. Kitchen Management

* Tracks active orders and individual order items.
* Enables kitchen staff to monitor and update order preparation status.

### 3. Menu Management

* Stores menu items, descriptions, prices and related attributes.
* Supports structured management of menu information.

### 4. Table Management

* Maintains restaurant table information and availability.
* Supports reservations and table-status tracking.

### 5. Employee Management

* Stores employee information and operational roles.
* Supports management of restaurant staff across different functions.

### 6. Customer Management

* Maintains customer records and feedback.
* Enables storage and retrieval of customer reviews.

### 7. Billing & Delivery

* Maintains invoice and billing records associated with orders.
* Stores delivery-related information and delivery representative records.

## Database Design

The system uses a **relational database model** with multiple interconnected entities. Primary and foreign keys establish relationships between customers, employees, menu items, orders, tables, invoices and delivery records while maintaining data consistency.

## Technologies

* MySQL
* SQL
* MySQL Workbench
* Relational Database Design

## Learning Outcomes

* Designed and structured a relational database for a multi-module business workflow.
* Applied SQL-based database operations for data creation, management and retrieval.
* Worked with entity relationships, primary keys, foreign keys and structured test data.
